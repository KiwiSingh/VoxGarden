# Changelog

## 2.0.0 — 2026-10-25

- Notes + lyrics workspace with MIDI import, editable syllable timing and velocity, overlapping-note support and monophonic audio transcription.
- In-plugin downloads for ten Supertonic 3 voices; English, Hindi, German, Japanese, Russian and Dutch pronunciation. Core ML GPU-preferred inference with CPU PSOLA pitch/timing.
- Exact MIDI, velocity-led and chord-root lyric modes. Common triads/sevenths support inversions; uncertain roots use bass. Chord modes share the leader word, so sequential words need sequential timing.
- Dry WAV drag to Live for auditioning through the current VoxGarden track before separately printing harmonies.
- Rose, Ocean and Sunset themes with light/dark modes; five interface/documentation languages.
- Floating lyrics window, selectable note rows, multi-selection and deletion.
- Existing plugin identity and 75 sound parameters retained; SnappySnapFX morphing and Live audition workflow user-confirmed.
- Known limitation: export Live MIDI clips to .mid before import. Neural singing requires Apple Silicon macOS 14+ and remains speech adapted into singing.

## 1.0.0

- 62 factory sounds, including original, female-choir/male-lead and male-choir/female-lead versions of eight raga textures.
- Key/scale-aware DSP harmony and optional local Seed-VC AI printing.
- Per-voice mute/solo, 1–8 voice count, dry input capture and aligned stem export.
- Light/dark themes and English, Hindi, German, Dutch and Russian interfaces.
- Stable earlier host parameter identities; 75 sound controls plus wrapper bypass.

See Ragas.md for note-set relationships and VALIDATION.txt for verification limits.
